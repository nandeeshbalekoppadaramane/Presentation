Executive Business Lessons

Key Strategic Insights from the Netflix Story
1. Self-Cannibalization Before Disruption

Netflix launched streaming in 2007 when DVD mailing was at peak profit, intentionally making its own legacy model obsolete before rivals could.

2. Vertical Integration for Moat Building

Licensing third-party content is a temporary distribution bridge; true defensive margin power comes from owning the IP (Originals).

3. The Math of Fixed Cost Amortization

In digital platforms with near-zero marginal distribution costs, audience scale is everything. 300M members pay for blockbusters no single studio can match.

4. Relentless Pricing & Monitization Flexibility

From DVD subscriptions to paid password sharing and $7/mo ad tiers, Netflix continually aligns monetization structures with customer segments.
